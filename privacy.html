Privacy Policy

AudioScope Music Lab · Effective date: October 8, 2026

In short: AudioScope Music Lab does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. The app has no internet permission, no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Everything it records or measures stays on your device until you choose to share or delete it.

Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the AudioScope Music Lab app for Android. For any question about it, write to feedback@fab-apps.com.

Information we collect

None. The developer does not receive any information from the app: no audio, no measurements, no usage statistics, no device identifiers and no crash reports.

The app does not declare the Android internet permission, so the operating system prevents it from connecting to any network.

Microphone

  • The microphone is the only permission the app requests.
  • It is activated only after you press a visible button to measure, tune or record.
  • Capture stops when you leave the app or turn off the screen. The app does not record in the background.
  • Audio is analyzed on the device. It is saved to a file only when you choose to keep the audio of a measurement or record a practice take.

What is stored on your device

The following is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read:

  • Measurements you save, with their spectra, notes and markers.
  • Audio recordings (WAV files) you choose to keep.
  • Calibration profiles, tuning and metronome presets, and your settings.

Sharing

Files leave the app only when you press an export button and pick a destination in Android's share dialog. From that point the file is subject to the terms of the app or service you chose.

The app does not share data with third parties and contains no advertising or analytics components.

Deleting your data

  • Every measurement and take can be deleted from within the app; its audio and related data are deleted with it.
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored.
  • Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to request or delete on our side.

Device backups

If Android's backup feature is enabled on your device, the system may include the app's data in your personal backup. That backup is managed by your device and your Google account, not by this app or its developer.

Children

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes any of the above, this page will be updated with a new effective date before that version is released.
